# Strategy — finding Viennese businesses that need us, and reaching them without sounding like everyone else

_2026-09-02 · owner David · status DRAFT for sign-off (decisions D1–D7 in [HUB.md](HUB.md))_

## 1. Thesis
Vienna has ~9,400 OSM-listed businesses in our target categories alone, and roughly half of them have either no website, a builder site from 2019, or a site that fails the phone test. Nobody is telling them *specifically* what is wrong. Every agency mail they get says "we do websites, SEO and social media".

We do the opposite of that mail. We look at one business, write down three checkable facts about its digital presence, put them on a page with a screenshot from a phone, name one offer with one price range, and send it by post with a person's name on it. The prospect can verify every sentence in 30 seconds. That is the whole trick: **diagnosis before offer, evidence before adjectives** (same rule as David's CV).

What we sell underneath is what David actually does: take a business that is not yet digital, or badly digital, give it a clear concept, a site or tool that works, and stay on to run it. Loutati (redesign → SEO → publishing retainer) and WOW Vienna (brand → site → digital operations) are the proof; the real-estate internal tools are the proof for the operations offer.

## 3. The Befund method (why it's not "just offering something general")
Every contact starts with a **Befund** (a finding), never with a pitch:

1. **Three facts they can check.** Generated by the audit, then hand-verified by the helper. Examples: "Kein mobiles Layout", "letztes Datum auf der Seite: 2021", "keine Terminbuchung, nur Telefon", "Seitentitel für Google leer". Facts, in German, one line each. No "your site could be more modern".
2. **One picture.** A screenshot of *their* homepage on a phone (390 px). Most owners have never seen it. This does more than any argument.
3. **One offer, one price range, one next step.** Mapped from the findings (see [OFFERS.md](OFFERS.md)). Not a menu. "Für Sie passt A2 Relaunch, 2.900–4.200 €, 4 Wochen. Nächster Schritt: 20-Minuten-Gespräch, Termin über den QR-Code."
4. **One proof.** The most similar thing we've done: a lawyer gets Loutati, an association gets WOW, a Hausverwaltung gets the internal tool story.
5. **A named sender and a real address.** A person, not a studio inbox.

Escalation layer (T4 in the hub): for the top 20 per batch, a **20-minute re-mock of their homepage** in our kit, printed as before/after. That is the "we already started" move; it's cheap for us (foundry kit + AI) and impossible for the mass mailers.

What we never do: "ganzheitliche Lösungen", "Full-Service-Agentur", "Digitalisierung", "Ihre Marke auf das nächste Level", lists of tools, "kostenloses Erstgespräch" as the headline, stock-photo brochures. Rules in [HANDBUCH.md](HANDBUCH.md) §6.

## 4. Pipeline (how the list is built)
`harvest → audit → score → human check → asset → send → log`. Details in [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md).

- **Harvest:** OpenStreetMap via Overpass (free, legal, has websites/phones/addresses for most Vienna businesses). Second sources later: WKO Firmen A-Z (626k Austrian companies, official), Herold, Google Maps.
- **Audit:** our script fetches each site and records facts (HTTPS, viewport, builder, last dates, SEO basics, booking, contact, English, tracking, speed, Impressum names).
- **Score:** facts → German Befund lines + score + offer id. Businesses **without** a site are their own segment (A1).
- **Human check:** the helper opens the top of each list on a phone, confirms the three lines, finds the decision-maker's name (Impressum, LinkedIn, WKO), marks `geprüft`.
- **No-site businesses** get a different letter: "Sie sind auf Google/OSM nur mit Adresse und Telefon zu finden. So sieht das aus: [screenshot of the empty Google result]."

## 5. Channels and the law (this changes the plan)
Austria is stricter than Germany. **§174 TKG 2021** prohibits electronic direct marketing (e-mail, SMS, messenger) *and* advertising calls **without prior consent, also to businesses**. The sender must be able to prove consent; the only exception is an existing customer relationship. Fines run into five figures, and Vienna professionals (lawyers!) know this law.

| Channel | Legal | Fit | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Letter by post** | ✅ fully legal | Highest: physical, designed, rare, verifiable | Primary. ~€1.50/piece all-in |
| **Walk-in / handover** | ✅ | Gastro, studios, practices with a reception | Primary for S3/S6 in walkable districts; the helper hands over the Befund card |
| **LinkedIn / Instagram DM** | ⚠️ platform message; legally arguable, tolerated in practice for one-to-one, non-bulk notes | Good for S1/S4 decision-makers | Secondary: a 3-line personal note, never a template blast |
| **Contact form on their site** | ⚠️ counts as electronic post; grey | Tempting, cheap | Decided no (D2): the risk lands in the reputation segment we want most |
| **Cold e-mail** | ❌ without consent | — | Never. Only after they reply ("Schicken Sie mir das per Mail") — then it's consented |
| **Cold call** | ❌ without consent | — | Never cold. Calls only as follow-up to a letter that invited a call *and* after a reply, or when they called us |
| **Warm intros** (Loutati's network, WOW's, IKG, Ani Poel restaurants) | ✅ | Best conversion of all | Ask every existing client for two names |

So the funnel is: **letter → (10 days) → second touch (LinkedIn note or walk-in) → reply → call → proposal within 48 h**. Every letter carries a QR to a personal page and a direct booking link (Calendly-style) so replying is one tap.

If after 200 letters the reply rate is under 3 %, the letter is wrong, not the list: change the Befund format, the proof, or the segment before scaling.

## 8. Risks and honesty
- **Audit is automatic.** False findings kill credibility in a segment built on reputation. Hand-check every line before it's printed.
- **Capacity.** Two won projects a month is already David's full delivery capacity next to existing clients. Batches stay at 50 until the first projects are delivered.
- **Price anchoring.** Loutati's retainer is €240/month; the draft catalogue is higher. Decide (D1) before the first letter, and don't undercut the catalogue in the letter.
- **German.** Letters, calls and proposals are in German. David's German is A2; the helper owns the German surface, David owns the design and the numbers.
- **Data protection.** The list holds business data (names from Impressum are public by law). Keep it in this vault, not in a third-party CRM, and delete rows on request.
