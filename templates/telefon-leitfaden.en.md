# Guide — phone (only on callback/reply; never cold, § 174 TKG)

**Goal of the call:** a 20-minute appointment with David. Not selling.

**Opening (callback on our letter):**
> "{Name} from {studio}, you reacted to our letter about {domain} – thank you. Do you have two minutes?"

**If they call:**
> "Good that you're calling. Which of the three points interested you most?"

**Five questions (any order, note all):**
1. Which point spoke to you?
2. Who decides on the website/tool at your company?
3. Is there a date by which it should be ready (season, move, opening, trade fair)?
4. What did the current site cost / who looks after it?
5. Does {day} at {time} suit you for 20 minutes with David – phone, video or at your place?

**Price question:** repeat the range from the letter. "You'll get the exact price in writing within 48 hours after the call."

**Close:**
> "I'll send the appointment confirmation to {e-mail} – is that okay?" → consent with date in `notiz`.

**Afterwards:** `status = gespräch`, answers to the five questions in `notiz`, appointment in David's calendar, short message to David with the website link and the five answers.

**Don't:** promise discounts, invent services, badmouth other providers, talk longer than ten minutes.
