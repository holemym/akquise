# Skeleton — written proposal (David, within 48 h after the call)

_Format: 2–3 pages PDF or an unlisted web page (model: `loutati-redesign/pitch.html` — reuse the structure, T3-4). German. No agency phrases._

1. **Starting point** — the three findings from the letter + what came up in the call (2–4 sentences, their words).
2. **Goal** — one sentence: what is different after the project (not "modern website" but "patients book online, even at 10 pm").
3. **What we do** — the one package from [../OFFERS.md](../OFFERS.md), broken into 5–8 concrete points. What's not included stands next to it.
4. **Process** — weeks 1–N, who delivers what by when (content, access, approvals).
5. **Proof** — the matching reference with link and one sentence on the result there.
6. **Price** — fixed price within the range, payment plan 50/50, VAT note, what the client pays directly (hosting/domain).
7. **Afterwards** — optional retainer (B1/A3/C3) in one paragraph, no pressure.
8. **Next step** — "A reply with 'fine' is enough; start on {date}." Valid 14 days.

Length: under 700 words. One image: the before/after sketch if one exists (T4-2).
