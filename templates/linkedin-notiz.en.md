# Template — LinkedIn/Instagram note (second touch, 10 days after the letter)

_Only individually, only to decision-makers, never in series. Three lines. No attachments, no link salad._

**Connection request (LinkedIn, max. 300 characters):**
> Hello {First name Last name}, last week I sent you a short finding about {domain} by post (three points, one phone screenshot). If it didn't arrive, I'm happy to send it as a link. {Name}

**Message after acceptance:**
> Thanks for connecting. The point that stood out most: {finding 1, one sentence}. We just solved that for {short reference} ({reference URL}). If you have 20 minutes, David will show you what it would look like for you: {booking link}.

**Instagram (restaurants/studios):**
> Hi {Name}, we dropped by on {day} and left a card – {finding 1 in one sentence}. If you like, we'll show you in 20 minutes what your site could look like on a phone. Best, {Name}

After every note: `kanal = brief+linkedin`, update `kontaktiert_am`. No second note without a reply.
