## Conditions (put in every proposal)
- **Payment:** 50 % on order, 50 % at go-live. Retainers monthly in advance. Payment term 14 days.
- **Included:** 2 revision rounds per stage; templates for Impressum/Datenschutz (client's lawyer signs off legal text); handover with 30-minute training; the client owns domain, hosting accounts, code and files.
- **Not included:** photography (we recommend photographers), final legal texts, paid ads budget, translations beyond DeepL-plus-edit, hosting/domain fees (typically €10–25/month, paid by the client directly).
- **Timeline** starts when we have content facts and access (domain, existing site). Waiting time on the client's side pauses the clock.
- **Scope creep:** anything outside the package is quoted separately before we do it; small things are absorbed.
- **VAT:** currently invoiced without USt (Kleinunternehmerregelung) — confirm and state on every invoice (D6).
- **Cancellation:** retainers with 1 month notice after the minimum term.
