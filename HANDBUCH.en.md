## 2. The idea: finding before offer
We never write "we build websites" to anyone. We look at *one* business, write down **three checkable facts** about its presence, add **a photo of its website on a phone**, name **one offer with a price range** and **one next step**. The recipient can verify every line in 30 seconds. That is the whole difference.

Example findings (they come automatically from our check; you confirm them):
- "No mobile layout – on a phone you have to zoom and drag."
- "Last visible date on the site: 2021."
- "No online booking – enquiries only by phone."
- "Page title/description for Google are empty."
- "German only – in the 1st district an English version is missing."

**Rule:** never send a finding you have not seen yourself on a phone or in the browser. One wrong finding to a lawyer costs us the whole district.

## 5. What we *don't* say (language rules)
Forbidden because it sounds like every other agency: "holistic", "full service", "digitalisation", "to the next level", "tailor-made solutions", "innovative", "state of the art", "free initial consultation" as the hook, "we are a young dynamic team", tool lists (Figma, WordPress…), superlatives.

Instead: concrete observation, concrete comparison, concrete number, concrete next step. Short sentences. Formal "Sie". No exclamation marks. No "I hope this e-mail finds you well".

## 6. How we like offers
A letter or message always has the same five parts, in this order:
1. **Reason** – one sentence why this business exactly: "I looked at your website because we just did the same for a law practice in the 2nd district."
2. **Finding** – three lines, checkable, plus photo.
3. **One offer** – name, what's inside (one sentence), price range, duration.
4. **One proof** – the most similar reference, one sentence, with address.
5. **One step** – "20 minutes on the phone or at your place, appointment via the QR code or reply to this address."

Never more than one A4 page. Never several offers in the first contact. Never "I'd be happy to present our portfolio".

## 7. What is legally allowed (important, Austria is strict)
**§ 174 TKG 2021:** advertising e-mails, SMS, messenger messages **and** advertising calls without prior consent are prohibited – **also to companies**. Fines up to five figures, and our target group (lawyers, tax advisors) knows this law.

| Channel | Allowed? | How we do it |
|---|---|---|
| Letter by post | ✅ | Main channel. Personally addressed, with a name |
| Walking in | ✅ | Restaurants, studios, practices with a reception: hand over the finding card, don't sell, just "have a look" |
| LinkedIn/Instagram message | ⚠️ | Only individually, personally, three lines, no series. Only to decision-makers |
| Contact form on their website | ❌ | Decided (D2): no — counts as electronic post, the risk lands in our most important segment |
| Cold e-mail | ❌ | Never. Only once someone replies or says "send it to me by e-mail" – then it's allowed, and you note the date |
| Cold call | ❌ | Never. Call only if announced in the letter **and** there was a reaction, or if they called us |
| Referral by existing clients | ✅ | Best channel. Ask every client for two names |

Every consent (e.g. "yes, e-mail me") is recorded with the date in the `notiz` column.

## 8. Weekly process
1. **Monday – list:** David approves the batch (50 businesses from `data/SHORTLIST.md`). You open each website on a phone, confirm or correct the three finding lines, look up the decision-maker's name (Impressum, LinkedIn, WKO Firmen A-Z) and set `status = geprüft`.
2. **Tuesday – material:** Claude generates screenshot + personal page (QR) per business. You write the letters from the template, David skims.
3. **Wednesday – dispatch:** print, sign, post. `status = gesendet`, `kanal = brief`, `kontaktiert_am`.
4. **Thursday/Friday – second touch** for the batch from 10 days ago: LinkedIn note or walk-in. `kanal = brief+linkedin`.
5. **Replies:** you answer within 24 hours in German, book the call into David's calendar, fill in `antwort`. David calls. The proposal follows within 48 hours after the call.
6. **Friday – numbers:** sent / replied / calls / proposals / won into the [HUB.md](HUB.md) session log.

Everything lives in `data/prospects_scored.csv` (columns `status, kanal, kontaktiert_am, antwort, notiz` are yours; Claude overwrites the other columns on the next run).

**Status values:** `neu → geprüft → gesendet → nachgefasst → antwort → gespräch → angebot → gewonnen / verloren / nicht-kontaktieren`

## 9. Qualification on the phone (when someone calls or calls back)
Five questions, then hand over:
1. What in the letter spoke to you? (which finding lands)
2. Who decides on the website/tool at your company?
3. Is there a date by which it should be ready?
4. What did you last spend on it / what did the current site cost?
5. When does a 20-minute call with David suit you?

You quote the price range from the letter if asked. Everything else: "David answers that in the call, he has already looked at your site."

## 10. Objections (first version, grows with real replies in INBOX.md)
- **"We already have someone."** – "Good. Then keep the finding, it's correct regardless. If the three points are still there in three months, get in touch."
- **"Too expensive."** – "The website check costs €190 and is credited. Then you know exactly what you need before spending anything."
- **"We don't need it, clients come by referral."** – "Then referrals still check you on Google first – and see exactly this [screenshot]."
- **"Send me something by e-mail."** – "Gladly, to which address?" → record consent, date.
- **"Where did you get my data?"** – "From your Impressum and public directories (OpenStreetMap, WKO). We only store company name, address and what's on your website. On request we delete the entry immediately."
